Assumptions

  • Ranges reflect typical Central Luzon pricing for local cabinet makers. They are not supplier prices.
  • The low end assumes laminated particle board and basic hardware. The high end assumes marine plywood carcasses, high-pressure laminate and soft-close hardware, which hold up better in humid rooms.
  • Cabinet prices include fabrication, delivery and installation. Electrical work assumes new points tap existing circuits without a new breaker.
  • Sizes come from the developer floor plan and an estimated 2.4 m ceiling. Final prices depend on measured dimensions.
  • A ready-made bunk bed can cut the built-in line by roughly a third, with less fitted storage.
