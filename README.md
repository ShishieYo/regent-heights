# Regent Heights

Design studies for our family house at Regent Heights (Alexa unit).

Live site: https://shishieyo.github.io/regent-heights/

- `3d/` — interactive 3D model of the whole house, both floors joined by the stair
- `bedrooms/` — redirects to `3d/`
- `budget/` — indicative cost estimate for the whole house
- `ground-floor/` — concept plan for the sala, kitchen, dining and parking

All layouts are preliminary. Dimensions come from the developer floor plan and the ceiling is estimated at about 2.4 m until measured on site.
