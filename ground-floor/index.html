Sala, kitchen and parking

A first layout read from the developer plan. Two things still need checking on site before this becomes final: where the kitchen counter actually sits, and how far it is from the front wall to the street.

Built-in Furniture Refrigerator Car

Sala

The TV goes on the solid party wall, on a floating 1.6 m console, so the stair foot stays clear. A 2 m sofa faces it about 1.65 m away, which suits a 50 to 55 inch TV. The sofa back also marks where the living area ends and dining begins.

Refrigerator

Put it at the end of the counter on the dining side, with its back to the party wall and the door opening toward the walkway. It stays within a step of the sink, never blocks the service-area door or the toilet, and you can grab a drink without walking into the cooking zone.

The service area is the fallback, but heat, humidity and rain exposure shorten a fridge's life there, so use it only if it is fully roofed.

Dining

A built-in banquette with drawers runs along the party wall, with a 120 × 75 cm table and two chairs. It seats your family of four, with the banquette taking an adult and a child. There is no chair at the fridge end, so the fridge door always opens freely.

Entry

A 30 cm deep shoe cabinet goes on the inside wall of the entry pocket. That leaves about 90 cm to walk in past the door.

Parking needs one measurement

The plan shows 3.50 m from the front wall to the lot line. A 2020 Vios is about 4.43 m long, so nose-in it sits roughly 1.2 m past that line.

  • Width is fine. The 3.65 m bay leaves about 90 cm for each door.
  • Measure from the front wall to the street curb. If it is 4.9 m or more, the car fits without blocking the road.
  • Parallel parking across the 5 m frontage is not practical, because you need far more room to swing in.
